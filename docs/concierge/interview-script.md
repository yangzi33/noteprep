# Concierge interview script (DRAFT)

Goal: test the assumptions A1–A5 from the strategy plan. Listen more than you talk. Ask for real past examples ("last time…"), not opinions. Record answers in a table, one row per tutor.

## Before the first call (10 min)
- Ask: grade levels and curricula you teach, number of active students, sessions per week, topics coming up in the next 2 weeks (this selects the packs you generate for them).

## Interview 1: current practice (20 min)
1. Walk me through how you prepared your last lesson. What did you open, make, print? (A1)
2. How long did it take, and how often per week? Which part is the most annoying? (A1)
3. Where do worksheets and answer keys come from today (own files, textbook, a generator, ChatGPT)? (A1, A2)
4. Has an answer key ever been wrong? What happened? How often? (A2)
5. If you use ChatGPT or a generator: what do you fix afterwards? (A2, A5)
6. Do you share notes or only worksheets with students? (A3)
7. How do you print or share materials (paper, screen share, PDF)? (A9)

## After each pack you receive (10 min)
- How long before you could use it? What did you change or skip? (A5)
- Would you hand it to a student as is? If not, why not? (A5)
- Which of the three documents did you open or print? (A3)
- Was the difficulty right for your student? Were the questions varied enough?
- Was there a question you wanted to swap or remove? (feeds the replace/remove feature)
- What topic did you need that we did not have? (feeds topic demand)

## Mockup test (once, 5 min)
Show the curriculum dropdown mockup (see mockup-test.md). Ask the question exactly as written there.

## Closing (each interview)
- What would make you use this every week? What would make you stop?
- Pricing, directional only: at what monthly price would this be too cheap to trust, a bargain, expensive, too expensive? (A10)
- Who else teaches like you and would try it?

## Tracking table columns
Tutor id (anonymous) | grades/curricula | prep time per week | current tools | wrong-key incident? | packs received | time to print | edits made | documents used | topics requested | would hand to student (Y/N) | price range

## Rules
- Record what tutors did, not what they said they would do.
- Do not ask leading questions or defend the product.
- Do not collect student personal information.
