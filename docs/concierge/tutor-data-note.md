## What NotePrep is
NotePrep helps independent math tutors prepare lessons. During early access the founder generates lesson packs for you and may ask you for feedback.

## What we collect
- Your email address, and the account created for you.
- The lesson requests you give us (topic, level, duration, difficulty) and the lesson packs generated from them.
- Your feedback: interview notes and your answers to our questions.
- Basic usage records, such as when a lesson was generated or downloaded.

## What we ask you NOT to give us
Please do not send student names, contact details, grades or any personal information about students. Describe students anonymously (for example "a Grade 9 student who struggles with negatives").
