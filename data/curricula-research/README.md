# Researched curriculum datasets (not shipped)

Datasets here are research output for review. They are NOT loaded into any database, NOT generated by `pnpm curricula:build`, and NOT part of the shipped catalogue in `data/curricula` (a unit test asserts that nothing here also exists there). `pnpm curriculum:validate data/curricula-research` applies every dataset rule, including the D-118 `requireSources` rules, and `tests/unit/curricula-research.test.ts` runs the same validation in CI.

| Dataset | Scope | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `uk-wales/suggested-2` | Wales, secondary maths, Years 7 to 13 | Three year bands, six courses (Years 7-9; GCSE Foundation and Higher as separate tiered courses; Level 2 Additional Mathematics; GCE Mathematics; GCE Further Mathematics). Every record carries a source, section, confidence and retrieval date. Topic wording is NotePrep's own. |

Promoting a dataset to the shipped catalogue needs an owner decision: the shipped datasets are generated from `data/curricula-src/curricula.json`, and integration tests assert exact dataset counts and `latestVersionId` values.
